01 | Recognition

Five signs your website is making the introduction harder

You do not need a full redesign to recognize the problem. These are the moments that make a qualified visitor hesitate.

Signal 01

The mobile experience feels cramped

Prospective clients often meet your practice on a phone. Important information should not require pinching, zooming, or hunting.

Signal 02

Your specialties are hard to understand

A visitor should quickly know who you help, what you help with, and whether you are accepting new clients.

Signal 03

Scheduling takes too many steps

The path from interest to consultation should be obvious, secure, and available outside office hours.

Signal 04

Updates depend on an old vendor

Changing a bio, service, or availability notice should not become a ticket that sits for weeks.

Signal 05

You cannot see what the site produces

The website should show inquiries, consults, and follow-up activity without requiring a marketing dashboard degree.
